TileIPTV privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

TileIPTV is a video player for playlists you provide. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not send any information to the developer.

What stays on your device

The playlists, logins, guide sources, favorites, reminders and settings you enter are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

What is sent, and to whom

  • To the playlist, guide and stream servers you add: requests to load channels, the guide and video, including any login those servers need. These servers are run by you or your provider, not by the developer, and their own privacy policies apply.
  • To Google Play: in-app purchases (TileIPTV Pro) are handled by Google Play Billing.
  • To your other devices: "Send to another device" sends your playlists and settings directly to the device you choose on your local network, protected by a 4-digit code.

Backups

A backup file contains your playlists and logins. It is saved where you choose; keep it private.

Children

TileIPTV is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
